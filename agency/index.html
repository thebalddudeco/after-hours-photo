A gathering becomes a story through the people in it.

People give an occasion its meaning. The conversations that find their own rhythm. A room gradually coming to life. The small exchanges that say more than a running order ever could.

At After Hours, we’re interested in that connection. We bring an editorial eye to the things you’ve planned and an open mind to everything that happens around them.

We work with brands, communities, and hosts who care about the experience they create. Together, we make photographs and videos that carry its character beyond the day itself.

Based in New York City and co-founded by Justin Von Braun and Steven Rodrigues, After Hours is a direct collaboration with the people behind the cameras. We get to know your occasion, your priorities, and the people who make it yours.
