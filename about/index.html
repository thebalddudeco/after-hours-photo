About After Hours

Good photographs start with feeling comfortable.

It’s easier to be yourself when the people behind the cameras feel familiar. Meet Justin and Steven, the co-founders of After Hours Photo.

The people make the picture.

A shared look, an unexpected reunion, someone finally letting loose. We’re drawn to the things that make a gathering feel personal, and we give them the same attention as the big moments.

Before your event, we’ll talk about the people you’re bringing together and what you hope to take away from it. That conversation helps us arrive with a sense of what matters, while leaving room for the things nobody could have planned.

Co-founder

Justin Von Braun

A photographer and videographer working across events, fashion, portraits, and commercial assignments. Justin brings an editorial eye and a hands-on approach from the initial brief to the final delivery.

Co-founder

Steven Rodrigues

A visual storyteller drawn to the rhythm, light, and character of a scene. Steven focuses on clean, timeless photographs that bring subtle moments and individual expression into view.
