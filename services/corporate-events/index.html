Planning questions

Can you cover both the brand and the guests?

Yes. Share the moments, people, branding and details that matter to your brief. We can discuss a balance of candid coverage, guest portraits and the event environment.

Can photography and short-form film be planned together?

Yes. Tell us where you intend to use the content and which moments are essential. Photography and motion requirements are scoped together before coverage is confirmed.

What should I send with an inquiry?

The date, venue or neighborhood, event schedule, approximate guest count, coverage hours and intended use. Include any access restrictions or must-have photographs.
