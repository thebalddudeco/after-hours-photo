Planning questions

Will you help guests who want a photograph together?

Yes. Coverage can combine candid moments with brief, directed portraits. Let us know if there are particular groups or family photographs you would like to make time for.

Can the coverage feel natural rather than posed throughout?

Yes. Share the atmosphere you want and the moments that matter. We can discuss how much direction you want and when to leave the gathering to unfold.

Does a consultation reserve the event date?

No. The 30-minute consultation is a planning call. Photographer availability, scope, pricing and the event booking are confirmed separately.
