Before we roll.

Can you create vertical and landscape edits?

We can plan for the formats you need. Share the intended platforms before the event so capture and editing can be scoped for those outputs.

How long is the film and when will it arrive?

Edit length and turnaround depend on the assignment. Your proposal confirms the agreed outputs, delivery schedule and revisions before you book.

What should I include in my inquiry?

Your date, location, schedule, approximate guest count, photo or video needs, intended use and any deadline. Include an available budget range or ask for guidance.
