Start with your date, location, schedule and the photographs you need. We will discuss guest count, access, key moments and how you intend to use the images. Justin will confirm availability, the photographer or team, and the proposed coverage before you book.

Planning questions

Can we share a schedule and a list of important guests?

Please do. A running order, key contacts, portrait priorities and any moments that cannot be repeated help shape the coverage plan.

Can you photograph arrivals and the celebration?

Yes. We can discuss the sequence from arrivals through the celebration, including the time and photographer coverage each part needs.

How is a quote prepared?

Share the schedule, location, intended use and photography or motion requirements. Coverage and pricing are agreed for the event before the booking is confirmed.
