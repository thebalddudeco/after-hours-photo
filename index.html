How it works

From the first brief
to the final delivery.

  1. Brief + plan

    Share your date, venue, schedule and priorities. Justin follows up with availability, coverage options and a clear proposal.

  2. Cover the event

    Before the day, we agree key people, moments, access and the coverage responsibilities. The plan makes room for the unscripted moments, too.

  3. Edit + deliver

    Your photographs and films are selected, edited and checked against the agreed brief, then delivered in the formats and timeframe confirmed for your booking.

Planning a series of events? Include the dates in your inquiry so we can discuss a consistent approach across your calendar.

09 / About After Hours

The people behind
the image.

Justin von Braun

Justin von Braun is the founder of After Hours Photo. Based in New York City, he brings an editorial approach to event photography and guides each inquiry from the initial brief to a proposed coverage plan. Photography, film, delivery and the assigned photographer or crew are confirmed before booking.

He works with people and brands who want strong images without feeling over-directed. Justin helps clients feel comfortable, finds the light, and stays ready for the moments that happen on their own.

That’s the goal: photographs that feel honest, look considered, and still feel like the people and places they came from.

Steven Rodrigues

Steven Rodrigues is a visual storyteller and a firm believer in the power of a great image. He sees every scene as having its own rhythm, light and character, then weaves those elements into clean, timeless visuals.

From concept through final edit, Steven turns subtle moments into images that elevate their subjects and reveal true character.

For him, a great image should stand out immediately and stay with you long after.
